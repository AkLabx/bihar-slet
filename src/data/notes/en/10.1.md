### Part 1: English Alphabet Positions & Coding-Decoding Principles

To achieve high speed and accuracy in reasoning sections, candidates must memorize both forward and backward place values of the English alphabet (A to Z):

#### A. The 'EJOTY' Benchmark Rule (Forward 5s):
$$E = 5, \quad J = 10, \quad O = 15, \quad T = 20, \quad Y = 25$$

#### B. Opposite Letter Pairs (Sum of Numerical Positions is Always 27):
* $A \leftrightarrow Z$ (AZad) $[1 + 26 = 27]$
* $B \leftrightarrow Y$ (BoY) $[2 + 25 = 27]$
* $C \leftrightarrow X$ (CruX) $[3 + 24 = 27]$
* $D \leftrightarrow W$ (DeW) $[4 + 23 = 27]$
* $E \leftrightarrow V$ (EVening) $[5 + 22 = 27]$
* $F \leftrightarrow U$ (FUll) $[6 + 21 = 27]$
* $G \leftrightarrow T$ (GT Road) $[7 + 20 = 27]$
* $H \leftrightarrow S$ (High School) $[8 + 19 = 27]$
* $I \leftrightarrow R$ (Indian Railway) $[9 + 18 = 27]$
* $J \leftrightarrow Q$ (Jungle Queen) $[10 + 17 = 27]$
* $K \leftrightarrow P$ (KanPur) $[11 + 16 = 27]$
* $L \leftrightarrow O$ (LOve) $[12 + 15 = 27]$
* $M \leftrightarrow N$ (MaN) $[13 + 14 = 27]$

#### C. Major Patterns in Coding-Decoding:
1. **Letter Shifting:** Adding or subtracting a uniform or progressive integer to letter positions ($+1, +2, +3...$ or $-1, -2...$).
2. **Reverse Letter Coding:** Substituting letters with their diametric opposite alphabet counterpart ($B \rightarrow Y, A \rightarrow Z$).
3. **Position Transposition / Jumbling:** Rearranging the physical sequence of characters without modifying their identity (e.g., reversing pairs or reversing whole words).
4. **Direct Numerical / Symbol Substitution:** Translating characters into assigned digits, place value sums, or product values (e.g., if $CAT = 3 + 1 + 20 = 24$).

### Part 2: Number & Alphabet Series

Frequently tested sequence patterns in competitive examinations:

| Sequence Pattern Type | Benchmark Example | Mathematical Logic & Derivation Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Arithmetic Difference Series** | 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ... | Common progressive step difference ($+5, +7, +9, +11$) or perfect squares $n^2$ |
| **Double Difference Series** | 2, 5, 11, 20, 32, ... | Tier 1 differences: 3, 6, 9, 12 (increasing by $+3$ each step); next term: $32 + 15 = 47$ |
| **Square & Cube Patterns** | 0, 7, 26, 63, 124, ... | $n^3 - 1$ sequence: $1^3-1, 2^3-1, 3^3-1, 4^3-1, 5^3-1$; next term: $6^3 - 1 = 215$ |
| **Prime Number Series** | 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, ... | Consecutive prime numbers only; next term: **19** |
| **Alternating Dual Series** | 10, 15, 12, 18, 14, 21, ... | Two interwoven series: $(10, 12, 14, 16)$ and $(15, 18, 21, 24)$ |
| **Multiplicative Combination ($n \times k \pm m$)** | 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ... | $\times 2 + 1$: $3 \times 2 + 1 = 7, 7 \times 2 + 1 = 15...$; next term: $63 \times 2 + 1 = 127$ |

### Part 3: Blood Relations & Family Tree Construction

Blood relation problems are resolved methodically by drawing a hierarchical Family Tree diagram:

```
        Grandparents (Paternal / Maternal) [Generation +2]
                        │
           Parents / Uncles / Aunts [Generation +1]
                        │
    [Self] ── (Siblings / Cousins / Spouse) [Generation 0]
                        │
            Children / Nephews / Nieces [Generation -1]
                        │
                   Grandchildren [Generation -2]
```

#### Standard Diagrammatic Notations:
* Male Member: $[+]$ or Square $\square$
* Female Member: $[-]$ or Circle $\bigcirc$
* Marital Relationship (Spouse): Double-headed arrow $\longleftrightarrow$
* Sibling Relationship: Single horizontal bond line $\text{---}$
* Generation Gap: Vertical descending line $\mid$

#### Core Kinship Terminology:
* Father's Father = Paternal Grandfather | Mother's Father = Maternal Grandfather
* Father's Mother = Paternal Grandmother | Mother's Mother = Maternal Grandmother
* Father's Brother = Paternal Uncle | Mother's Brother = Maternal Uncle
* Brother's Son = Nephew | Sister's Son = Nephew
* Brother's Daughter = Niece | Sister's Daughter = Niece
* Spouse's Sister = Sister-in-law | Spouse's Brother = Brother-in-law

### Part 4: Direction and Distance Sense

#### A. The Eight Cardinal and Intercardinal Directions:
```
                         North (N)
                             ↑
     North-West (NW)         │        North-East (NE)
                ↖            │            ↗
                  ↖          │          ↗
       West (W) ←────────────┼────────────→ East (E)
                  ↙          │          ↘
                ↙            │            ↘
     South-West (SW)         │        South-East (SE)
                             ↓
                         South (S)
```

#### B. Foundational Direction Rules:
1. **Right Turn:** Always rotate **$90^\circ$ Clockwise**.
2. **Left Turn:** Always rotate **$90^\circ$ Counter-Clockwise (Anticlockwise)**.
3. **Shortest Direct Distance:** Whenever a right-angled triangle is formed, apply the **Pythagoras Theorem**:
   $$H = \sqrt{P^2 + B^2}$$
   *(Hypotenuse = $\sqrt{\text{Perpendicular}^2 + \text{Base}^2}$)*
4. **Shadow Concepts:**
   * **At Sunrise:** The Sun is in the East $\rightarrow$ Shadows of all objects fall toward the **West**.
     * If a person faces North at sunrise, their shadow falls to their **Left**.
   * **At Sunset:** The Sun is in the West $\rightarrow$ Shadows of all objects fall toward the **East**.
     * If a person faces North at sunset, their shadow falls to their **Right**.
   * **At 12:00 Noon:** The Sun is positioned overhead vertically, hence no perceptible shadow is cast.

### Part 5: Order and Ranking Formulas

#### A. Position of a Single Person from Both Ends:
If person $A$ occupies rank $L$ from the left (or top) and rank $R$ from the right (or bottom) in a linear row:
$$\text{Total Persons} = L + R - 1$$
$$\text{Rank from Right (R)} = (\text{Total} - L) + 1$$
$$\text{Rank from Left (L)} = (\text{Total} - R) + 1$$

#### B. Position Interchange Problems:
When two persons $A$ and $B$ swap their respective seats:
1. The displacement in rank magnitude is identical for both individuals.
2. **Total Persons** = (New position of one person + Original position of the other person from opposite end) $- 1$.
3. **Persons seated between them** = $\text{Magnitude of rank difference} - 1$.
