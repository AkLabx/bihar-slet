# Unit 1 — Foundations of Library Science

## Sub-Unit 1.1: Types of Libraries, Functions and Objectives

> **Comprehensive English Study Notes**

### Introduction

Libraries are broadly classified into four major categories based on their target users, nature of reading materials, funding sources, and institutional objectives:

1. **Academic Libraries**
2. **Public Libraries**
3. **Special Libraries**
4. **National Library**

---

### 1. Academic Libraries

**Definition:**  
Libraries attached to formal academic institutions (schools, colleges, and universities) serving the educational, curricular, instructional, and research needs of students, teachers, and researchers.

#### Major Categories:

1. **School Library:**
   * **Objective:** Inculcate reading habits and develop self-study skills among primary, secondary, and higher secondary students.
   * **Commission Recommendation:** **Mudaliar Commission / Secondary Education Commission (1952–53)** recommended revitalizing school libraries, appointing full-time trained librarians, and making the Open Access System compulsory.

2. **College Library:**
   * **Objective:** Provide prescribed curriculum textbooks, reference books, academic journals, and competitive examination materials suited to undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) courses.

3. **University Library:**
   * **Objective:** Support advanced learning, specialized knowledge creation, and original research.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exam POV Key Commissions & Recommendations:**
> * **Radhakrishnan Commission (University Education Commission, 1948–49):**  
>   * Famously described the university library as the **"Heart of the University"**.  
>   * *Dr. S. Radhakrishnan stated: "The library is the heart of all the university's work; directly so as regards its research work, and indirectly as regards its educational work."*
> * **Kothari Commission (National Education Commission, 1964–66):**  
>   * Headed by Dr. D.S. Kothari, it recommended that every university should allocate **6.5% to 10%** of its total academic budget for library development.
> * **Ranganathan UGC Library Committee (1957):**  
>   * Chaired by Dr. S.R. Ranganathan, this committee recommended a library grant of Rs. 15 per student and Rs. 200 per teacher/researcher, along with faculty status and salary parity for university library staff.

### 2. Public Libraries

**Definition:**  
Referred to as the **"People's University"**, a public library provides democratic, free, and unrestricted access to knowledge and information for all citizens regardless of age, race, gender, religion, language, or social status.

#### Sources of Finance:
* Annual budgetary grants from state governments.
* **Library Cess:** A surcharge levied on property tax, house tax, vehicle tax, etc. (applicable in states where Public Library Acts have been enacted with cess provisions).
* **RRRLF (Raja Rammohun Roy Library Foundation):** Established in **May 1972 (Kolkata)** under the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, as the apex body for promoting, supporting, and financing public library systems across India.

#### UNESCO Public Library Manifesto:
* **First Published:** **1949** (by UNESCO).
* **Revisions:**
  * **1972:** Revised on the occasion of UNESCO's 25th anniversary.
  * **1994:** Revised in Paris in collaboration with IFLA (widely cited version).
  * **2022 (Latest):** Released in July 2022 by IFLA and UNESCO, incorporating digital inclusion, UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), media literacy, and intellectual freedom.

#### Historical Milestones & Institutions:
* **Baroda State Library Movement (1910):**  
  The modern public library movement in India was pioneered by **Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III** in Baroda State. He invited American library pioneer **W.A. Borden** to serve as the Director of the State Library Department.
* **Delhi Public Library (DPL):**  
  * **Year of Establishment:** **1951**.  
  * Established as a joint pilot project between the Government of India and **UNESCO** to serve as South Asia's first model public library. Inaugurated on October 27, 1951, by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru.
* **Special Historical Libraries in Bihar:**
  1. **Khuda Bakhsh Oriental Public Library, Patna:**  
     * Established: **1891** by Khan Bahadur Maulvi Khuda Bakhsh.  
     * Declared an **'Institute of National Importance'** by an Act of Parliament in **1969**. It houses a world-renowned collection of rare Arabic, Persian, and Urdu manuscripts.
  2. **Radhika Sinha Institute and Sachchidananda Sinha Library, Patna (Sinha Library):**  
     * Established: **1924** by Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha.  
     * Designated by the Government of Bihar as the official **'State Central Library of Bihar'**.
  3. **Bihar Public Libraries and Information Centres Act:**  
     * Enacted in **2008** by the Bihar Legislature (making Bihar the 16th state in India to pass library legislation).

### 3. Special Libraries

**Definition:**  
Libraries established to serve a specific parent organization or a specialized client group (scientists, doctors, engineers, economists, policymakers, or corporate professionals) focusing on a single specialized subject domain to fulfill quick, pinpointed information requirements.

#### Nature of Collection:
* Rather than comprehensive general books, the collection predominantly consists of **Micro-documents**:
  * Primary research journals and periodicals
  * Patents and technical standards (e.g., ISI, BIS, ISO, ASTM)
  * Research and technical reports
  * Conference proceedings and trade literature

#### Key Services:
1. **CAS (Current Awareness Service):**  
   Provides rapid awareness of the latest published literature, tables of contents, and indexes to subject specialists.
2. **SDI (Selective Dissemination of Information):**  
   * **Pioneer:** **H.P. Luhn (1958)**, an IBM scientist.  
   * **Mechanism:** Automated computerized matching of a **User Interest Profile** with a newly acquired **Document Profile** to route only relevant, pinpointed information directly to the user.

#### Major Associations & Indian Examples:
* **International and National Associations:**
  * **IASLIC** (Indian Association of Special Libraries and Information Centres): Established **September 3, 1955**, Headquarters: Kolkata.
  * **SLA** (Special Libraries Association): Established **1909**, USA (Founder: John Cotton Dana).
  * **ASLIB** (Association of Special Libraries and Information Bureaux): Established **1924**, UK.
* **Prominent Indian Special Libraries:**
  * **BARC Library** (Bhabha Atomic Research Centre, Mumbai)
  * **ICAR / IARI Library** (Indian Council of Agricultural Research, New Delhi)
  * **NML** (National Medical Library, New Delhi)
  * **NIEPVD Braille Library** (National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Visual Disabilities, Dehradun - India's premier braille library for visually impaired patrons).

### 4. National Library

**Definition:**  
The apex library of a nation, funded and administered by the central government, legally charged with collecting, preserving, and maintaining the nation's entire published intellectual output and cultural heritage for present and future generations.

#### Legal Deposit Legislation in India:
* **Act:** **Delivery of Books and Newspapers Act, 1954**.
  * **1954:** Originally enacted for books.
  * **1956:** Amended to include newspapers and periodicals.
* **Legal Mandate:** Every publisher in India is legally obligated to deliver, at their own expense, within **30 days** of publication, one copy of each published item to each of the **4 designated Depository Libraries** of India.

#### The 4 Depository Libraries of India:
1. **National Library of India, Kolkata**
2. **Connemara Public Library, Chennai**
3. **The Asiatic Society Central Library, Mumbai**
4. **Delhi Public Library, Delhi**

#### Indian National Bibliography (INB):
* **Compiling & Publishing Authority:** **Central Reference Library (CRL), Kolkata**.
* **First Publication:** Compilation commenced in **1957**; first quarterly and annual volumes published in **1958**.
* **Base Collection:** Books deposited under the Delivery of Books Act at the National Library, Kolkata.
* **Father of INB:** **B.S. Kesavan** is revered as the 'Father of the Indian National Bibliography'.

#### Historical Evolution of the National Library of India (Kolkata):
* **1836 (Calcutta Public Library):** Established on a proprietary basis (moved to Metcalfe Hall in 1844).
* **1891 (Imperial Library):** Formed by amalgamating several government secretariat libraries.
* **1902–1903 (Lord Curzon's Initiative):** Viceroy Lord Curzon passed the Imperial Library Act 1902, amalgamating Calcutta Public Library and Imperial Library. Formally opened to the public at Metcalfe Hall on **January 30, 1903**.
  * *First Librarian:* **John Macfarlane**.
  * *First Indian Librarian:* **Harinath De (1907)**.
* **1948 (Change of Name Act):** Post-independence, the Imperial Library (Change of Name) Act 1948 rechristened it as the **National Library of India**.
* **February 1, 1953 (Official Dedication):** Dedicated to the nation at the Belvedere Estate (Alipore, Kolkata) by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, the Union Minister of Education.
  * *First National Librarian of Independent India:* **B.S. Kesavan**.

### 5. Comparative Matrix for Examinations

| Feature / Dimension | Academic Library | Public Library | Special Library | National Library |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Users** | Students, teachers, and researchers | All members of society without restriction | Specialists, scientists, corporate researchers | The entire nation, researchers, and global scholars |
| **Nature of Collection** | Textbooks, reference monographs, theses | General books, fiction, popular periodicals | Micro-documents: patents, standards, journals | Complete national published output |
| **Primary Objective** | Support curriculum and research | Lifelong learning, civic info, recreation | Pinpointed, rapid information retrieval | Permanent preservation of national intellectual heritage |
| **Funding Source** | Parent institution, UGC, state education dept | State grants, library cess, RRRLF | Parent organization, corporate/R&D funds | Central Government (Ministry of Culture) |
| **Distinctive Services** | User orientation, Book Bank, Inter-Library Loan | Mobile libraries, children's wings | SDI (H.P. Luhn, 1958), CAS, technical translations | Legal deposit maintenance, compiling INB |

### 6. High-Yield Exam Points & One-Liners

1. **Heart of the University:**  
   Coined by the Radhakrishnan Commission (University Education Commission, 1948–49).

   Kothari Commission (1964–66) recommended allocating **6.5% to 10%** of total university budget to libraries.
3. **People's University:**  
   Universal designation for the Public Library.
4. **UNESCO Public Library Manifesto Editions:**  
   **1949** (original), **1972**, **1994** (with IFLA), and **2022 (latest edition)**.
5. **Pioneer of Public Library Movement in India:**  
   Maharaja Sayajirao Gaekwad III of Baroda (1910) with American specialist W.A. Borden.
6. **Delivery of Books Act Timeline:**  
   Passed in **1954**; amended in **1956** to include newspapers and periodicals.
7. **The 4 Depository Libraries:**  
   (1) National Library Kolkata, (2) Connemara Public Library Chennai, (3) Asiatic Society Mumbai, (4) Delhi Public Library Delhi.
8. **Indian National Bibliography (INB):**  
   Compiled and published by **Central Reference Library (CRL), Kolkata** since 1957; Father of INB is **B.S. Kesavan**.
9. **Selective Dissemination of Information (SDI):**  
   Pioneered by **H.P. Luhn (1958)**; cornerstone service of special libraries.
10. **State Central Library of Bihar:**  
    Sachchidananda Sinha Library (Radhika Sinha Institute), Patna (Est. 1924).
