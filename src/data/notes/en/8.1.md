### Part 2: The Preamble of the Constitution

* **Opening Declaration:** "WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a **SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC** and to secure to all its citizens: JUSTICE, LIBERTY, EQUALITY, and FRATERNITY..."
* **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976:** The Preamble has been amended only once till date, adding three words:
  1. **Socialist**
  2. **Secular**
  3. **Integrity**
* **Judicial Status:** In the historic *Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)* case, the Supreme Court ruled that the Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution and can be amended under Article 368 without altering the 'Basic Structure'.

### Part 3: Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12 to 35)

Inspired by the **US Constitution (Bill of Rights)**. Originally, the Constitution provided 7 Fundamental Rights. However, the **44th Amendment Act, 1978** abolished the 'Right to Property' (Article 31) as a Fundamental Right and reclassified it as a **Legal/Constitutional Right under Article 300A**. Presently, there are **6 Fundamental Rights**:

```
                       ┌─── 1. Right to Equality [Articles 14–18]
                       ├─── 2. Right to Freedom [Articles 19–22]
                       ├─── 3. Right against Exploitation [Articles 23–24]
6 Fundamental Rights ──┼─── 4. Right to Freedom of Religion [Articles 25–28]
                       ├─── 5. Cultural and Educational Rights [Articles 29–30]
                       └─── 6. Right to Constitutional Remedies [Article 32]
```

#### High-Yield Articles:
* **Article 14:** Equality before the law and equal protection of the laws.
* **Article 17:** **Abolition of Untouchability** — practice of untouchability in any form is forbidden and punishable by law.
* **Article 19(1)(a):** Freedom of speech and expression (freedom of the press is implicit within it).
* **Article 21:** Protection of life and personal liberty (Right to Life, Dignity, and Right to Privacy).
* **Article 21A:** **Right to Education (RTE)** — provides free and compulsory education to all children aged 6 to 14 years (inserted by the **86th Amendment Act, 2002**).
* **Article 24:** Prohibition of employment of children below the age of 14 years in factories, mines, or hazardous occupations.
* **Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies):**
  * Termed the **"Heart and Soul of the Constitution"** by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.
  * Empowers the Supreme Court (Article 32) and High Courts (Article 226) to issue **5 Types of Prerogative Writs**:
    1. *Habeas Corpus* ("To have the body") — against unlawful detention.
    2. *Mandamus* ("We command") — directs a public official to perform statutory duties.
    3. *Prohibition* — issued by a superior court to prevent an inferior court from exceeding jurisdiction.
    4. *Certiorari* ("To be certified") — quashes an order passed by an inferior court/tribunal without jurisdiction.
    5. *Quo-Warranto* ("By what authority") — prevents illegal usurpation of a public office.

### Part 4: Directive Principles of State Policy & Fundamental Duties

#### A. Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Articles 36 to 51)
* **Source:** Borrowed from the **Constitution of Ireland** (which borrowed from Spain).
* **Nature:** Non-justiciable (not legally enforceable in courts of law), yet fundamental in the governance of the country.
* **Key Articles:**
  * **Article 40:** **Organization of Village Panchayats** (Gandhian philosophy).
  * **Article 44:** **Uniform Civil Code (UCC)** for all citizens throughout India.
  * **Article 45:** Early childhood care and education for all children until age six.
  * **Article 48A:** Protection and improvement of environment and safeguarding of forests and wildlife.
  * **Article 50:** Separation of Judiciary from the Executive.
  * **Article 51:** Promotion of international peace and security.

#### B. Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, Article 51A)
* **Source:** Inspired by the Constitution of the erstwhile **USSR**.
* **Origin:** Recommended by the **Sardar Swaran Singh Committee**; 10 duties were added by the **42nd Amendment Act, 1976**.
* **11th Duty:** Added by the **86th Amendment Act, 2002** (duty of parents/guardians to provide opportunities for education to children aged 6 to 14).
* Total count: **11 Fundamental Duties**.

### Part 5: Union and State Governance & Bihar Legislature

#### A. The President and State Governor
* **President (Articles 52–62):** First citizen of India and formal Executive Head. Minimum age: **35 years**. Elected by an electoral college consisting of elected members of both houses of Parliament and elected members of Legislative Assemblies.
* **Governor (Articles 153–162):** Constitutional Head of the State. Appointed by the President and holds office during the pleasure of the President. Minimum age: **35 years**.

#### B. Parliament & Bihar Legislative Allocation

| Legislative Body | Total Seats | Bihar Seat Allocation | Minimum Eligibility Age | Normal Tenure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lok Sabha** | 543 | **40 Seats** (6 seats reserved for SC: Gopalganj, Hajipur, Samastipur, Sasaram, Gaya, Jamui) | 25 Years | 5 Years |
| **Rajya Sabha** | 245 | **16 Seats** | 30 Years | Permanent Body (6-year member term, 1/3rd retire every 2 years) |

*(Note: In India, only 6 states have bicameral legislatures: Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana).*

### Part 6: Panchayati Raj System & Bihar's Historic 50% Women Reservation

#### A. Constitutional Evolution
* **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957):** Recommended a **3-tier Panchayati Raj structure** (Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, and Zilla Parishad) for democratic decentralization.
  * **First State to Adopt:** **Rajasthan** inaugurated the Panchayati Raj on **October 2, 1959**, in Nagaur district by Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru.
  * **Second State:** Andhra Pradesh (1959).
* **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 (Effective April 24, 1993):**
  * Conferred constitutional status on Panchayats (**April 24 is commemorated as National Panchayati Raj Day**).
  * Inserted **Part IX** and the **11th Schedule** into the Constitution, enumerating **29 functional subjects** for Panchayats.
* **74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992:** Conferred constitutional status on Urban Local Bodies / Municipalities (Part IX-A, 12th Schedule, 18 subjects).

#### B. Bihar Panchayati Raj Act, 2006
* **Trailblazing Affirmative Action:** Bihar became the **First State in India** to grant **50% Reservation for Women** in Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs) and Urban Local Bodies.
* **3-Tier Structure:**
  1. *Gram Level:* Gram Panchayat (Mukhiya / Ward Members) — serving ~7,000 population.
  2. *Block Level:* Panchayat Samiti (Pramukh) — serving ~5,000 population per member.
  3. *District Level:* Zilla Parishad (Zilla Parishad Chairperson) — serving ~50,000 population per member.
* **EBC Reservation:** Bihar also granted **20% reservation for Extremely Backward Classes (EBCs)** in Panchayati Raj institutions.
