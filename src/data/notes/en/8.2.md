### Part 2: Everyday Chemistry

#### A. Acids, Bases & The pH Scale
* **Discovery of pH Scale:** Formulated in 1909 by Danish biochemist **S.P.L. Sorensen**.
  * $\text{pH} = 7$: Neutral (Pure distilled water).
  * $\text{pH} < 7$: Acidic (Lower values indicate stronger acidity).
  * $\text{pH} > 7$: Basic / Alkaline (Higher values indicate stronger alkalinity).
* **pH Values of Common Physiological Solutions:**
  * **Human Blood:** **7.35 – 7.45 (Slightly alkaline)**
  * Human Saliva: 6.5 – 7.5
  * Gastric Juice: 1.5 – 2.0 (High acidity due to concentrated Hydrochloric Acid - $\text{HCl}$)
  * Household Vinegar: 2.5 – 3.0
  * Seawater: ~8.5
* **Naturally Occurring Organic Acids:**
  * Vinegar $\rightarrow$ **Acetic acid** ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$)
  * Oranges & Lemons $\rightarrow$ **Citric acid**
  * Tamarind & Unripe Mangoes $\rightarrow$ **Tartaric acid**
  * Tomatoes & Spinach $\rightarrow$ **Oxalic acid** (Kidney stones are formed predominantly of Calcium Oxalate)
  * Sour Milk / Curd $\rightarrow$ **Lactic acid**
  * Ant & Bee Stings $\rightarrow$ **Formic acid / Methanoic acid** ($\text{HCOOH}$)

### Part 3: Biology, Human Physiology & Health

#### A. Cellular Organelles
* Basic structural and functional unit of life is the **Cell** (Discovered by Robert Hooke in 1665).
* **Mitochondria:** 'Powerhouse of the Cell' — site of cellular respiration and ATP synthesis.
* **Ribosomes:** 'Protein Factory of the Cell' — translates RNA into polypeptide chains.
* **Lysosomes:** 'Suicide Bags' — contains hydrolytic digestive enzymes that break down waste.
* **Chloroplasts:** 'Kitchen of the Plant Cell' — site of photosynthesis containing chlorophyll (a complex porphyrin ring coordinating a central **Magnesium** ion).

#### B. Blood Groups & Circulatory Physiology
* Blood is a specialized fluid connective tissue.
* **ABO Blood Groups:** Discovered by Austrian physician **Karl Landsteiner** in 1900 (awarded Nobel Prize in 1930).
* **O Negative ($O^-$):** **Universal Red Blood Cell Donor** — lacks A, B, and Rh surface antigens.
* **AB Positive ($AB^+$):** **Universal Blood Recipient** — lacks anti-A, anti-B antibodies in plasma.
* **Hemoglobin:** Primary respiratory oxygen transport metalloprotein (contains **Iron, $\text{Fe}^{2+}$**).

#### C. Vitamins & Deficiency Disorders

| Vitamin | Chemical Name | Solubility | Classical Deficiency Disorder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vitamin A** | Retinol | Fat-soluble | **Night Blindness (Nyctalopia)**, Xerophthalmia |
| **Vitamin B1** | Thiamine | Water-soluble | **Beri-Beri** (Prevalent with consumption of polished rice) |
| **Vitamin B2** | Riboflavin | Water-soluble | Cheilosis (Fissuring at mouth angles) |
| **Vitamin B3** | Niacin / Nicotinic Acid | Water-soluble | Pellagra (Characterized by 4D syndrome: Dermatitis, Diarrhea, Dementia, Death) |

| **Vitamin C** | **Ascorbic Acid** | Water-soluble | **Scurvy (Bleeding spongy gums)**; Essential for collagen synthesis; absent in milk |
| **Vitamin D** | Calciferol | Fat-soluble | **Rickets** in children, Osteomalacia in adults |
| **Vitamin E** | Tocopherol | Fat-soluble | **Impaired fertility / Reproductive failure**; known as 'Beauty Vitamin' |
| **Vitamin K** | Phylloquinone | Fat-soluble | **Defective blood clotting / Severe hemorrhage** |

*(Memory Trick: Water-soluble = B and C; Fat-soluble = K, E, D, A).*

#### D. Infectious Diseases & Causative Pathogens
* **Bacterial Infections:** Tuberculosis (caused by *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*; treated with BCG vaccine), Cholera (*Vibrio cholerae*), Typhoid (*Salmonella typhi*; diagnosed via Widal Test), Tetanus (*Clostridium tetani*), Plague, Diphtheria.
* **Viral Infections:**
  * **AIDS:** Caused by Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV); diagnosed via **ELISA test** and Western Blot test.
  * Polio: Caused by Poliovirus (Salk injectable vaccine; Sabin oral drops).
  * Rabies: Hydrophobia virus (Rabies vaccine discovered by Louis Pasteur).
  * Dengue Fever: Flavivirus transmitted by female *Aedes aegypti* mosquito (sharp drop in blood platelets).
* **Protozoan Infections:**
  * **Malaria:** Caused by *Plasmodium* parasite; transmitted by female *Anopheles* mosquito; discovered by Sir Ronald Ross; treated with Quinine extracted from the bark of the Cinchona tree.
  * **Kala-azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis):** Caused by *Leishmania donovani*; vector is the **Sandfly** (*Phlebotomus*); endemic to the river plains of Bihar.

### Part 4: Ecology, Biodiversity & Climate Change

* **Ecosystem Concept:** Term coined in 1935 by British ecologist **A.G. Tansley**.
  * *Lindeman's 10% Trophic Efficiency Law:* On average, only 10% of energy transferred between trophic levels is assimilated into biomass.
* **Greenhouse Effect & Global Warming:**
  * Major greenhouse gases: **Water Vapor ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$), Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$), Methane ($\text{CH}_4$), Nitrous Oxide ($\text{N}_2\text{O}$), Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), and Tropospheric Ozone ($\text{O}_3$)**.
  * Gas contributing most to cumulative anthropogenic global warming: $\text{CO}_2$.
* **Stratospheric Ozone Layer Depletion:**
  * Ozone layer is located in the **Stratosphere**, absorbing solar ultraviolet-B (UV-B) radiation.
  * Unit of thickness: **Dobson Unit (DU)**.
  * Primary depleting agents: Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) from refrigerants and aerosol propellants.
  * **Montreal Protocol (1987):** International environmental treaty to phase out ozone-depleting substances (effective 1989).
  * **World Ozone Day:** Observed annually on **September 16**.
* **Global Environmental Observances:**
  * **World Environment Day:** **June 5** (Commemorating the 1972 Stockholm Conference).
  * **Earth Day:** **April 22**.
  * **International Day for Biological Diversity:** **May 22**.
