### Part 1: Syllogism: Rules & Venn Diagram Methodology

In categorical syllogisms, statements (premises) must be accepted as indisputably true, regardless of real-world facts. A candidate must determine which conclusion(s) logically and definitively follow.

#### A. Four Standard Categorical Propositions:
1. **'A' Type (Universal Affirmative - All):** "All $A$ are $B$."
   * Immediate Valid Inferences: Some $A$ are $B$; Some $B$ are $A$.
2. **'E' Type (Universal Negative - No):** "No $A$ is $B$."
   * Immediate Valid Inferences: No $B$ is $A$; Some $A$ are not $B$; Some $B$ are not $A$.
3. **'I' Type (Particular Affirmative - Some):** "Some $A$ are $B$."
   * Immediate Valid Inferences: Some $B$ are $A$.
4. **'O' Type (Particular Negative - Some Not):** "Some $A$ are not $B$."
   * No immediate converse conversion is universally valid.

#### B. Venn Diagram Rules for Syllogism:
* Construct the primary Venn diagram illustrating **Minimum Overlapping** between given sets.
* A definitive conclusion is classified as **True** if and only if it holds valid across **all conceivable overlapping diagrams**.

#### C. Complementary Pairs / 'Either-Or' Conditions:
The conclusion 'Either I or II follows' requires three compulsory criteria:
1. Both independent conclusions must be individually doubtful / unverified from the basic diagram.
2. Both conclusions must possess identical subject and predicate elements.
3. One conclusion must be Affirmative and the other Negative:
   * **Pair 1:** 'Some' + 'No' (e.g., Some cats are dogs + No cat is a dog).
   * **Pair 2:** 'All' + 'Some not' (e.g., All pens are books + Some pens are not books).

### Part 3: Mathematical Operations & The BODMAS Rule

When mathematical operators ($+, -, \times, \div$) are swapped or encoded with arbitrary symbols, equations must be evaluated strictly adhering to the **BODMAS** sequence:

$$B \rightarrow O \rightarrow D \rightarrow M \rightarrow A \rightarrow S$$
* **B (Brackets):** Remove brackets in order: Vinculum / Bar $(\bar{x})$, Parentheses $()$, Braces $\{\}$, and Square Brackets $[]$.
* **O (Of / Orders):** Powers, exponents, and square roots.
* **D (Division):** Division operations $(\div)$.
* **M (Multiplication):** Multiplication operations $(\times)$.
* **A (Addition):** Addition operations $(+)$.
* **S (Subtraction):** Subtraction operations $(-)$.

> [!CAUTION]
> Performing addition before division or multiplication invalidates arithmetic equality. Always compute Division and Multiplication prior to Addition and Subtraction.

### Part 4: Missing Numbers in Figures & Numerical Matrices

Standard derivation methods for resolving $3 \times 3$ matrices or concentric numerical shapes:
1. **Constant Row / Column Sum:** Sum of values across rows or columns is uniform ($\text{Row}_1 = \text{Row}_2 = \text{Row}_3 = K$).
2. **Operations on First Two Terms Generating the Third:**
   * $A \times B + C$ or $(A + B) \times C$
   * $A^2 + B^2 = C$ or $\sqrt{A} + \sqrt{B} = C$
3. **Diametric Opposite Pairing:** Values opposite each other follow polynomial progressions (e.g., $x \rightarrow x^3 + 1$ or $x^2 + k$).

### Part 5: Mirror & Water Images (Non-Verbal Reasoning)

#### A. Mirror Images (Vertical Plane Inversion):
* Fundamental Principle: **Lateral Inversion** occurs (Left $\leftrightarrow$ Right swap), while vertical orientation (Top and Bottom) remains **strictly unchanged**.
* **11 Capital Letters of the English Alphabet that Remain Identical in a Vertical Mirror:**
  $$\mathbf{A, \quad H, \quad I, \quad M, \quad O, \quad T, \quad U, \quad V, \quad W, \quad X, \quad Y}$$
* **Clock Mirror Image Shortcut:**
  * To determine the mirror time of an analog clock, subtract the given time from **11:60 (or 23:60)**.
  * *Example:* If actual time is 8:20, mirror image time = $11:60 - 8:20 = \mathbf{3:40}$.

#### B. Water Images (Horizontal Plane Inversion):
* Fundamental Principle: **Vertical Inversion** occurs (Top $\leftrightarrow$ Bottom swap), while lateral orientation (Left and Right) remains **strictly unchanged**.
* **8 Capital Letters that Remain Identical in a Water Reflection:**
  $$\mathbf{C, \quad D, \quad E, \quad H, \quad I, \quad K, \quad O, \quad X}$$
* **Clock Water Image Shortcut:**
  * Subtract the given clock time from **18:30 (or 17:90)**.

### Part 6: Dice & Cube Principles

* **Standard Dice:** The sum of digits on any two opposite faces is always equal to **7**:
  $$1 \leftrightarrow 6, \quad 2 \leftrightarrow 5, \quad 3 \leftrightarrow 4$$
* **General Dice (Common Face Rule):**
  * When two different perspectives of a single die share exactly one common face, rotating clockwise from that shared digit establishes the respective opposite face pairings.
