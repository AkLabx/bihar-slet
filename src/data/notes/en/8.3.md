### Part 1: Flagship Welfare & Development Schemes of Bihar

#### A. 'Atmanirbhar Bihar Ke Saat Nishchay-2' (2020–2025/2026)
Formulated by Chief Minister Nitish Kumar under the Good Governance agenda, Saat Nishchay-2 focuses on seven strategic growth pillars:

```
                          ┌─── 1. Yuva Shakti - Bihar Ki Pragati (Mega Skill Centers, ITI/Polytechnic Centers of Excellence)
                          ├─── 2. Sashakt Mahila - Saksham Mahila (Higher Education Aid: Intermediate Rs 25,000, Graduation Rs 50,000)
                          ├─── 3. Har Khet Tak Sinchai Ka Pani (Irrigation water access to every farm)
 Saat Nishchay-2 (2020-25) ────┼─── 4. Swachh Gaon - Samriddh Gaon (Solar street lights in all villages, solid-liquid waste management)
                          ├─── 5. Swachh Shahar - Viksit Shahar (Urban solid waste processing, senior citizen shelters, Mokshadham)
                          ├─── 6. Sulabh Samparkata (Construction of bypasses, elevated flyovers & widening rural roads)
                          └─── 7. Sabke Liye Atirikt Swasthya Suvidha (Telemedicine, 'Bhavya' digital portal, Bal Hriday Yojana)
```

#### B. Women Empowerment & Financial Inclusion Schemes
* **Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana:** Lifecycle financial assistance to girls from infancy to university graduation.
  * Intermediate (Class 12) unmarried female pass-outs receive: **Rs 25,000**.
  * Graduation (Bachelor's Degree) female pass-outs (married or unmarried) receive: **Rs 50,000**.
* **Mukhyamantri Mahila Udyami Yojana:** Provides up to **Rs 10 Lakh financial assistance** for women entrepreneurs setting up MSMEs (Rs 5 Lakh grant + Rs 5 Lakh interest-free loan at 0% interest).

#### C. Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan
* **Launch Date:** October 2, 2019, on the 150th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.
* **Objective:** Comprehensive environmental mission tackling climate change through rejuvenation of traditional water bodies (pynes, aahars, stepwells, ponds), extensive afforestation, and rooftop solar power.
* **Global Recognition:** Highly lauded on international platforms by the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP).

#### D. Ganga Water Supply Scheme (Har Ghar Gangajal)
* **Inauguration:** November 27, 2022 (Rajgir) and November 28, 2022 (Gaya and Bodh Gaya).
* **Engineering Marvel:** Lifts surplus floodwaters of the Ganga from Hathidah (Mokama) and transports it through a 151-km-long underground transmission pipeline to water-stressed southern cities (**Rajgir, Gaya, Bodh Gaya, and Nawada**) for purified municipal tap water supply.
* **Significance:** India's first-ever green infrastructure inter-basin water transfer project.

#### E. Gayaji Rubber Dam
* **Location:** Built across the holy **Falgu River** near Vishnupad Temple in Gaya.
* **Key Feature:** **Largest Rubber Dam in India** — measuring 411 meters in length and 3 meters in height.
* **Engineering Technology:** Designed using Austrian expertise with technical assistance from IIT Roorkee. Ensures pilgrims have a continuous 2 to 3 feet water depth year-round for Pinda Daan and sacred bathing.

### Part 3: National Current Affairs Highlights

#### A. Chandrayaan-3 Historic Lunar Landing
* **Launch Date & Vehicle:** July 14, 2023, aboard the **LVM3-M4** launch vehicle from Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC-SHAR), Sriharikota.
* **Historic Landing:** On **August 23, 2023** at 18:04 IST, the Vikram Lander performed a historic soft landing near the **South Pole of the Moon**.
* **Global Distinction:** India became the **First nation in the world** to land near the lunar South Pole and the **Fourth nation globally** to soft-land on the Moon (after USA, Russia, and China).
* **Shiv Shakti Point:** The touchdown site of Vikram Lander was officially designated 'Shiv Shakti Point' by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
* **National Space Day:** Commemorating this monumental scientific triumph, **August 23** was declared **National Space Day**.
* **Robotic Rover:** 'Pragyan' — a 6-wheeled solar-powered scientific rover.

#### B. India's New Parliament House
* **Inauguration:** Dedicated to the nation on May 28, 2023, by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
* **Project Details:** Part of the Central Vista Redevelopment Project (Chief Architect: **Dr. Bimal Patel**; Constructed by: Tata Projects Ltd).
* **Geometry:** Triangular architectural design maximizing spatial ergonomics.
* **Chamber Capacity:** Lok Sabha chamber accommodates 888 seats (interior design theme: **National Bird Peacock**); Rajya Sabha chamber accommodates 384 seats (theme: **National Flower Lotus**).
* **The Sengol:** Sacred golden scepter originating from the Chola imperial tradition, symbolizing righteousness and transfer of authority, installed beside the Lok Sabha Speaker's chair.

#### C. Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023)
* Enacts **33% reservation for women** in the Lok Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies, and National Capital Territory of Delhi.
* The first historic constitutional amendment bill passed in the New Parliament House.

### Part 4: Major International Highlights

* **G20 New Delhi Leaders' Summit 2023:**
  * Venue: **Bharat Mandapam**, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi (September 9–10, 2023).
  * Summit Theme: *"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam"* — **"One Earth, One Family, One Future"**.
